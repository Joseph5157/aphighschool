### englishAbstract

G.O.Ms.No.35, School Education (Services.II) Department, dated 03.11.2026, makes the Andhra
Pradesh School Educational Subordinate Service Adhoc Rules, 2026 for the teachers and others of
Government schools and offices of the School Education Department. They supersede the adhoc
rules of G.O.Ms.No.11 (23.01.2009) and are deemed in force from 02.10.2026. The order records
that the rules were reissued to align with the local-cadre organisation under the Presidential
Order, 2025. Teachers in Mandal and Zilla Parishad schools are covered by a separate order,
G.O.Ms.No.36.
