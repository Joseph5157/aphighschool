### englishAbstract

DSC-2026 Societies Notification No.03/DSC-TRC-1/2026, dated 05.11.2026, invites online
applications under G.O.Ms.No.50 for Principal, PGT, TGT, Physical Director and Physical
Education Teacher posts across AP Model Schools, MJPAPBCWREIS, APREIS, APSWREIS, APTWREIS
(Gurukulam) and the Department for the Welfare of Differently Abled schools; the posts on offer
differ by society. Applications run from 11 to 30 November 2026 at ₹1,000 per post, and the
computer-based test starts on 20 December 2026. Society-wise vacancies are in the notification PDF.
