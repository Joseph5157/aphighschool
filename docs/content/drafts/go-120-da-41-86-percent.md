### englishAbstract

G.O.Ms.No.120, Finance (HR.VI-PC&TA) Department, dated 11-09-2026, raises Dearness Allowance
for Andhra Pradesh Government employees on the Revised Pay Scales, 2022 from 40.04% to 41.86% of
basic pay with effect from 01-01-2025. It extends to local-body employees, including teachers in
Zilla Parishad, Mandal Parishad and municipal schools, and to aided-institution staff on regular
RPS-2022 scales. The increase is paid in cash from the September 2026 salary. Arrears for
January 2025 to August 2026 are paid in four parts in 2028.

<ul>
  <li>Order: G.O.Ms.No.120, Finance (HR.VI-PC&amp;TA) Department, dated 11-09-2026.</li>

  <li>Applies to employees on the Revised Pay Scales, 2022.</li>
  <li>Cash payment starts with the September 2026 salary, paid in October 2026.</li>
  <li>Arrears period: 01-01-2025 to 31-08-2026 (20 months).</li>

<h2>Who is covered</h2>
<p>State Government employees on the Revised Pay Scales, 2022, and employees of Zilla Parishads, Mandal Parishads, Gram Panchayats, Municipalities and Municipal Corporations drawing a regular RPS-2022 scale. This includes teachers in those schools. Teaching and non-teaching staff of aided institutions on regular RPS-2022 scales are also covered.</p>

<h2>How the arrears will be paid</h2>
<ul>
  <li>10% of the arrears in January 2028.</li>
  <li>The remaining 90% in three equal instalments in March, May and July 2028.</li>
  <li>OPS employees: credited to the GPF account.</li>
  <li>CPS employees: on the first 10%, the arrears are credited to the PRAN account with the Government share; the remaining instalments are paid in cash.</li>
  <li>Employees who retired or retire between 01-01-2025 and 31-12-2027: arrears paid in cash.</li>
</ul>
