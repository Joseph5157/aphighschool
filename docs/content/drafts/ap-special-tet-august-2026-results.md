### englishAbstract

The School Education Department has released the results of the Special APTET – August 2026
(Notification dated 24.08.2026), a TET held only for in-service teachers. The computer-based
test was held from 11 to 15 October 2026, and results were declared on the final key.
Candidates can see their results through the candidate login on the official portal. A Special
TET pass satisfies the TET requirement for continuing in the present post or for promotion
only; it cannot be used for any future direct recruitment.

<h2>Who the Special TET was for</h2>
<p>In-service teachers, regular and those appointed on Minimum Time Scale, working in Government, local body, APREIS, Government welfare residential society, AP Model Schools and private aided schools, and in schools of other Government departments.</p>

<h2>What a pass is valid for</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Valid: meeting the mandatory TET condition for continuing in your present post, or for promotion to the next higher post.</li>
  <li>Not valid: any future direct recruitment. For that you need the regular APTET or CTET.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why this test was held</h2>
<p>The notification records that the Supreme Court held TET mandatory for the in-service teachers concerned, extended the time for compliance to 31 August 2028, and asked for TET to be held periodically, preferably twice a year. The Government then permitted a Special TET for in-service teachers alongside the regular TET.</p>
