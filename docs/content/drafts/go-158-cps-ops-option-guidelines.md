### englishAbstract

G.O.Ms.No.158, Finance (HR.III-Pension & GPF) Department, dated 09.11.2026, issues the
operational guidelines for the one-time option under G.O.Ms.No.53 (13-07-2026), which lets
employees recruited against a notification issued before 01.09.2004 but appointed on or after
that date, and now under CPS, move to the Old Pension Scheme. Serving and retired employees and
family pension beneficiaries must opt by 31.12.2026, only through the NIDHI portal. The option
is final, and accepted employees are admitted to GPF.

<h2>At a glance</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Order: G.O.Ms.No.158, Finance (HR.III-Pension &amp; GPF) Department, dated 09.11.2026.</li>
  <li>Implements: G.O.Ms.No.53, dated 13-07-2026, the one-time option policy.</li>
  <li>Last date to exercise the option: 31.12.2026.</li>
  <li>Who can apply: serving employees, retired employees and family pension beneficiaries who meet the GO 53 conditions.</li>
  <li>How: only through the NIDHI portal, using the "CPS-OPS Conversion" tile.</li>
  <li>The option is final and cannot be withdrawn.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Who is eligible</h2>
<p>All three conditions must be met: the recruitment notification or advertisement for your post was issued before 01.09.2004; you were appointed against it on or after 01.09.2004; and you were under CPS when G.O.Ms.No.53 was issued. The date that matters is the date of the recruitment notification.</p>

<h2>Step 1: get certified copies</h2>
<p>Apply to the authority that first appointed you for certified copies of four documents: the recruitment notification or advertisement, the selection list, your appointment order, and the order admitting you to duty. The GO asks that authority to issue them within 15 working days.</p>

<h2>Step 2: apply on NIDHI</h2>
<p>Submit your application on NIDHI to the Drawing and Disbursing Officer of your present office, uploading the certified copies. Retired applicants and family members apply to the DDO of the office where the employee last worked, after registering new NIDHI credentials.</p>

<h2>Step 3: verification and orders</h2>
<ol>
  <li>The DDO forwards the application to your initial appointing authority, which confirms on NIDHI whether the copies are authentic.</li>
  <li>The DDO and Head of Office verify it against your Service Register and recommend it.</li>
  <li>The Director of Treasuries and Accounts, or the Pay and Accounts Officer for Secretariat staff, scrutinises the proposal.</li>
  <li>The competent authority issues final orders.</li>
</ol>
<p>If your application is returned or recommended for rejection, you can fix the problem and resubmit before the deadline.</p>

<h2>After approval</h2>
<ul>
  <li>You are admitted to GPF, ZPPF or the applicable provident fund, and CPS deductions stop once PF subscription starts.</li>
  <li>Your Service Register records that you are governed by the AP Revised Pension Rules, 1980.</li>
  <li>You do not receive any part of the CPS corpus in cash. Your own contribution is credited to your GPF account, and the Government contribution and returns go back to the Government. Separate rules apply to retirees and death cases.</li>
</ul>

<h3>What if I do nothing?</h3>
