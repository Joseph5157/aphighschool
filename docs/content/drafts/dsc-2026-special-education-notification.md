### englishAbstract

DSC-2026 Special Education Notification No.02/DSC-TRC-1/2026, dated 05.11.2026, invites online
applications for School Assistant (Special Education) and Secondary Grade Teacher (Special
Education) posts in Government, Zilla Parishad, Mandal Parishad, Municipality and Municipal
Corporation schools, under G.O.Ms.No.51. Applications and fee payment run from 11 to 30
November 2026 at ₹1,000 per post. The computer-based test starts on 20 December 2026 and is in
English and Telugu only. District-wise vacancies are in the notification PDF.
