### englishAbstract

G.O.Ms.No.36, School Education (Services.II) Department, dated 03.11.2026, makes the Andhra
Pradesh School Educational Subordinate Service Adhoc Rules, 2026 for teachers working in Mandal
Praja Parishad and Zilla Praja Parishad schools. They supersede the adhoc rules of
G.O.Ms.No.12 (23.01.2009) and are deemed in force from 02.10.2026, reissued to align with the
local-cadre organisation under the Presidential Order, 2025. Government-school teachers are
covered separately by G.O.Ms.No.35.
