### englishAbstract

G.O.Ms.No.119, Finance (HR.VI-PC&TA) Department, dated 11-09-2026, raises Dearness Allowance
for employees on the Revised Pay Scales, 2022 from 37.31% to 40.04% of basic pay with effect
from 01-07-2024, including local-body school teachers and aided staff on regular RPS-2022
scales. The increase is paid in cash from the September 2026 salary. Arrears for July 2024 to
August 2026 are paid in four parts in 2027. The next instalment is G.O.Ms.No.120.
