### englishAbstract

G.O.Ms.No.22, School Education (Services.II) Department, dated 20.05.2025, issues the Andhra
Pradesh State Teachers (Regulation of Transfers) Rules, 2025 under the AP State Teachers
Transfers Regulation Act, 2025. The rules cover Headmasters Grade-II, School Assistants,
Secondary Grade Teachers and equivalent cadres in Government, Zilla Parishad, Mandal Parishad,
Municipal and Municipal Corporation schools, came into force immediately and supersede earlier
transfer rules and guidelines. They set compulsory-transfer and request-transfer tenure
conditions counted as on 31 May of the transfer year.

<h2>Who must be transferred</h2>
<ul>
  <li>A Headmaster Grade-II who has completed five academic years in the same school.</li>
  <li>Any other teacher who has completed eight academic years in the same school.</li>
  <li>A male Headmaster Grade-II or teacher below 50 years of age working in a girls' high school. If no woman teacher is available, a male teacher above 50 may be considered for such a school.</li>
</ul>
<p>Nine months of service in an academic year counts as a full academic year for these conditions.</p>

<h2>Who may request a transfer</h2>
<p>A Headmaster Grade-II or teacher who has completed at least two academic years in a school can apply for a request transfer.</p>

<h2>Who is protected</h2>
<p>A Headmaster Grade-II or teacher due to retire within two years is not transferred except on their own request.</p>

<p>The GO sets the standing rules. The dates of any transfer cycle, the counselling portal and vacancy lists are announced separately. Entitlement points, preferred categories and other detailed criteria are in the GO itself.</p>
