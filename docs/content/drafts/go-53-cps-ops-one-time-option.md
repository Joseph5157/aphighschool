### englishAbstract

G.O.Ms.No.53, Finance (HR.III-Pension & GPF) Department, dated 13-07-2026, adopts the
Government of India policy and gives a one-time option to State employees now under CPS to move
to the Old Pension Scheme, if their recruitment notification or advertisement was issued before
01.09.2004 and they were appointed on or after that date. The option is final and irrevocable.
Operational guidelines and the 31.12.2026 deadline were issued later in G.O.Ms.No.158.
