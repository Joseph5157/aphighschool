### englishAbstract

DSC-2026 vacancies are published in three separate detailed notifications dated 05.11.2026:
regular School Assistant and Secondary Grade Teacher posts, Special Education posts, and
Societies posts. The regular and Special Education notifications give vacancies by district and
management; the Societies notification gives them by society or management. The brief
notifications of 13.10.2026 carried tentative figures only.
