### englishAbstract

The School Education Department has published a revised suggestive syllabus for DSC-2026
(APTRT-2026) on the official portal, organised post by post, with a separate revised syllabus
for Special Education posts. For School Assistant and Secondary Grade Teacher posts, the
selection uses 100 marks: 80 for the computer-based test and 20 for APTET/CTET weightage; SA
(Physical Education) is assessed on the test alone. Part-wise marks are in the official
Information Bulletins.

  <li>One revised syllabus PDF covers the general posts post by post: SGT, School Assistant subjects, TGT, PGT and others.</li>

<p>Societies posts (Principal, PGT, TGT, PD, PET) are also covered in the revised syllabus, but their test structure is set out in the Societies Information Bulletin.</p>

<p>The School Assistant test has parts on general knowledge and current affairs, perspectives in education, classroom implications of educational psychology, the content of the subject, and the methodology of the subject.
