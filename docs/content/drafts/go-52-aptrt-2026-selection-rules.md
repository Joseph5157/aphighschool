### englishAbstract

G.O.Ms.No.52, School Education (Ser.I) Department, dated 04.11.2026, makes the Andhra Pradesh
Teacher Recruitment Test (APTRT/DSC-2026) Scheme of Selection Rules, 2026 for School Assistant
and Secondary Grade Teacher posts in Government, Zilla Parishad, Mandal Parishad, Municipality
and Municipal Corporation schools. The rules took effect immediately and name the District
Educational Officer as appointing authority. The regular DSC-2026 notification of 05.11.2026
implements this order. Special Education and Societies posts are governed by G.O.Ms.No.51 and
G.O.Ms.No.50.
