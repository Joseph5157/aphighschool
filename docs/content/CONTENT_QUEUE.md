## Event-driven posts

Create these only when the corresponding official action is live:

- application link;
- correction window;
- hall ticket;
- response sheet;
- provisional or final answer key;
- result or scorecard;
- web counselling;
- district or management list.

Do not pre-publish a dead action button. When an action closes, update its lifecycle state and
remove or relabel the call to action.

## Research completion definition

A topic is ready for drafting only when the pack contains:

- exact official issuer, document/page name, reference, date and URL;
- verified scope and exclusions;
- current action state and expiry trigger;
- safe English and Telugu search wording;
- unresolved claims explicitly excluded;
- Related Orders considered;
- a source-by-source fact ledger.
