## Teacher intent and terminology

- Primary questions: Which service rules apply to my school? What replaced the 2009 adhoc rules?
  From when are the new rules effective?
- English phrases: AP teachers service rules 2026, GO 35 adhoc rules, GO 36 MPP ZPP teachers rules.
- Telugu phrases: ఉపాధ్యాయుల సర్వీస్ రూల్స్ 2026, జీవో 35 అడ్‌హాక్ రూల్స్, జీవో 36 మండల జిల్లా పరిషత్ ఉపాధ్యాయులు.
- Keep management separate: Government schools, MPP/ZPP schools, Municipalities, Municipal
  Corporations, the two named corporations and AP Model Schools each have their own GO.
- "Adhoc Rules" is the official title for GO 35 and GO 36. Do not call them "special rules".
