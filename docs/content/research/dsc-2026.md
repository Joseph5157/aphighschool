### Disputed or unclear wording, not to be published

- O10 para 20 says qualifications must be held "as on the date of issue of this detailed
  Notification … i.e., 04.11.2026", but O10 itself is dated 05.11.2026. The Societies
  notification (O11 para 22) uses 05.11.2026. Do not state a qualification cut-off date for the
  Special Education stream until this is clarified; tell readers to follow the notification.
- The Societies schedule table (O11) loses its row layout on text extraction, so the mock-test
  and hall-ticket dates are not safe to publish (the same reason as in the regular draft).
