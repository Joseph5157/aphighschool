- Locate the exact AP Finance/GOIR order and confirm department, GO number and date.
- Verify employee DA and pensioner DR separately.
- Verify the effective date, cash-payment date and arrears period from the order.
- Locate any separate implementation, instalment or treasury instructions.
- Prepare calculator rules and official worked examples only after the order is approved.
- Find existing CMS post IDs for the order chain.

## Do not publish

- Any DA/DR rate, effective date or payment date from a competitor page
- A GO number without the official PDF
- Copied arrears tables or pay-scale matrices
- A calculator that has not been reconciled against verified order rules and test cases
- A claim that DA and DR use the same order without evidence
