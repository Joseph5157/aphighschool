## Teacher intent and terminology

- Primary questions: Am I eligible to switch from CPS to OPS? What is the last date? How do I
  apply and what documents do I need?
- English phrases: CPS to OPS AP GO 53, CPS OPS conversion GO 158, old pension option 2003 DSC.
- Telugu phrases: సీపీఎస్ నుంచి ఓపీఎస్ ఆప్షన్, పాత పెన్షన్ పథకం వన్ టైమ్ ఆప్షన్, జీవో 158.
- Keep "notification/advertisement issued before 01.09.2004" distinct from "appointed after".
  The condition is about the recruitment notification date, not the selection or result date.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | The option is for employees whose recruitment notification/advertisement was issued before 01.09.2004, who were appointed against it on or after 01.09.2004, and who are under CPS as on the date of GO 53 | O1 para 5(i)–(ii); O2 Annexure-I para 1(a) | All categories covered by G.O.Ms.No.653 of 2004 (O1 para 4(II)) | no | yes |
| F2 | The option, once exercised, is final and irrevocable; those who do not opt stay under CPS | O1 para 5(iv)–(v) | — | no | yes |
| F3 | Serving or retired employees and family pension beneficiaries must exercise the option on or before 31.12.2026 | O2 para 3 | GO 53 para 5(iii) gave three months "or such further period as may be notified"; GO 158 sets 31.12.2026 | yes | yes |
| F4 | Applications are made only through the NIDHI portal, using a "CPS-OPS Conversion" tile; serving employees use their current NIDHI credentials, and non-serving applicants register new credentials | O2 Annexure-I para 1(b)–(c) | Live status of the tile not verified (login required) | yes | yes, without an action button |
| F5 | Applicants first obtain certified copies of four documents from their initial appointing authority: the recruitment notification, the selection list, the appointment order and the joining order | O2 Annexure-I para 2(a) | — | no | yes |
| F6 | The application is submitted on NIDHI to the DDO of the present office (or the last office for non-serving applicants); the initial appointing authority confirms authenticity, the Head of Office recommends, and the DTA/PAO scrutinises before the competent authority issues final orders | O2 Annexure-I paras 2–4 | — | no | yes, as a sequence |
| F7 | A rejected or returned application can be resubmitted within the deadline after fixing the discrepancy | O2 Annexure-I paras 3(a)(ii), 3(b)(iv), 4(d) | — | yes | yes |

| F9 | Conversion gives a serving employee no right to any part of the CPS corpus in cash; the employee's contribution goes to GPF and the Government contribution and appreciation are adjusted to Government | O2 Annexure-I para 8–9 | Retiree and death cases have separate rules (paras 10–12) | no | yes, prose only |

- The NIDHI "CPS-OPS Conversion" tile cannot be confirmed live without credentials.
- The School Education Department memo forwarding GO 158 (reported by competitors) is not
  located on an official source; do not cite it.
- The detailed Annexure II/III formats are in GO 158; do not reproduce them.

- Corpus or pension amount calculations
- The unlocated School Education memo
