## Gaps and blockers

- Obtain the exact CSE proceeding number, date and official PDF.
- Confirm which cadres, managements and districts it covers.
- Confirm the profile-update, provisional-list, objection and final-list sequence.
- Obtain official district or department list URLs and publication dates.
- Determine whether SA and HM promotions use separate proceedings and lists.
- Locate governing service rules and existing CMS Related Order records.

## Do not publish

- Competitor-transcribed dates, lists or instructions
- A generic statewide list assembled from unofficial district spreadsheets
- An objection deadline or TIS action link without the official proceeding
- A final-list label on a provisional or undated list
- Mixed management or district claims
