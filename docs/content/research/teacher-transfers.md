## Teacher intent and terminology

- Primary questions: What are the current transfer rules? Is a transfer schedule active? Where is
  counselling? What applies to inter-district, mutual or spouse cases?
- English phrases: AP teacher transfers latest GO, GO 22 teacher transfer rules, teacher transfer
  web counselling, inter-district mutual spouse transfers.
- Telugu phrases: ఉపాధ్యాయ బదిలీల తాజా జీవో, జీవో 22 బదిలీ నిబంధనలు,
  టీచర్ వెబ్ కౌన్సెలింగ్, అంతర్ జిల్లా పరస్పర స్పౌస్ బదిలీలు.
- Do not merge Transfers with Rationalisation. GO 22 references rational deployment and GO 21,
  but each topic needs its own verified document scope.

## Gaps and blockers

- Search GOIR/CSE for amendments issued after 20 May 2025.
- Locate the official copy of G.O.Ms.No.21 and confirm its relationship to GO 22.
- Verify any inter-district, mutual or spouse-transfer order separately.
- Verify a current schedule and authorised counselling URL before creating a 2026 action page.
- Find existing CMS post IDs for Related Orders.

## Do not publish

- `AP Teacher Transfers 2026` as an active process without an exact order
- A counselling button derived from an old cycle
- Copied entitlement-point or vacancy tables
- A broad claim that every teacher cadre or management is covered
- Spouse, mutual and inter-district conditions merged without official support
